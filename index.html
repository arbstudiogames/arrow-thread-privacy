Arrow Thread – Privacy Policy

Developer: Arb Studio · Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Package: com.arbstudio.arrowthread

Arrow Thread ("the game", "we", "us") is a free puzzle game for Android published by Arb Studio. This policy explains what information the game uses, why it is used, who it is shared with, and what choices you have. We keep it short because the game collects very little.

1. Who this game is for

Arrow Thread is intended for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a person under 16 has used the game, please contact us (see section 11) and we will help.

2. Information we collect ourselves

We do not run a server for this game and we do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

The game stores a small save file only on your device (for example: your current level in each mode, best level, number of hints, and your settings such as sound, vibration and reminders). This file is never sent to us. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Ads appear in three ways: a banner on the home screen, a full-screen ad between some levels (not in the first levels), and optional "watch an ad" buttons that give a reward such as an extra hint or an extra heart. Rewarded ads are only shown if you tap those buttons.

To request, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the game may collect and use, under Google's own policies:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (and other device identifiers)Serving and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, fraud prevention
IP address (used to estimate a general location)Serving ads suitable for your region, fraud prevention
App interactions (ad views and taps)Measuring ad performance and paying for ads
Device and app diagnostic information (device model, OS version, SDK performance)Keeping ads working correctly

This information is handled by Google, not by us, and is transmitted over an encrypted connection. We do not sell your data. We never receive your advertising ID or any profile of you from Google. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and read Google's own privacy policy here: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Your advertising choices

  • Consent form. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form (a Google User Messaging Platform form) so you can choose how your data is used for ads. The game also asks you to accept this policy when you first open it, before any advertising service is started.
  • Ad personalisation and advertising ID. You can opt out of personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Changing your consent choice later. You can clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Thread → Storage → Clear data) to see the consent form again, or contact us and we will help.

5. Reminder notifications

If you allow it, the game can show occasional reminder notifications (for example, "Level 12 is waiting"). These are created on your device using Android's local notification feature. They do not use a server, do not use your personal data, and are never sent to us. The game asks for notification permission only once, after you finish an early level. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings (Reminders switch) or in Android's notification settings. Reminders are never scheduled between 10 PM and 9 AM, and they are cancelled whenever you open the game.

6. Permissions

  • Internet access / network state – needed only so ads can be loaded.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob as described in section 3.
  • Notifications – used only for the optional reminders in section 5.

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. The only third party that receives data because of the game is Google (through the AdMob SDK, as described above). The game is distributed through Google Play, which has its own privacy practices. The game may open this policy page in your browser when you tap the privacy button.

8. Security and retention

Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to retain, export or delete. The save file on your device is protected by Android's normal app sandbox. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or privacy laws in some other regions), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use for advertising, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since we do not collect or store personal data about you, most of these rights are handled by Google for the advertising data described in section 3. You can also contact us at any time (section 11) and we will help you use these rights.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change (for example, if we add new features or new advertising partners), we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, contact Arb Studio at: arbstudiogames@gmail.com
