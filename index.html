1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android game Arrow Thread (the "Game"), published by Arb Studio ("Arb Studio", "we", "us" or "our"). It explains what information the Game uses, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

The Game is free to play. It is supported by advertising served by Google AdMob. By installing and using the Game you confirm that you have read this policy. When you first open the Game it also shows a short notice asking you to accept this policy before any advertising service is started.

2. Who the Game is for

Arrow Thread is intended for people aged 16 and over. It is not designed for, or directed to, children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16, and the Game is not part of Google Play's Families programme.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 16 has been using the Game, please contact us (section 16) and, where possible, remove the Game from the device. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, there is nothing on our side to delete; for advertising data held by Google, see sections 5 and 13.

3. Information we do not collect

Arb Studio does not operate a server for the Game and the Game has no user accounts, log-in, chat, social features or in-app purchases. We do not collect, receive or store:

  • your name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address or date of birth;
  • your contacts, calendar, messages, photos, videos, files or microphone/camera data;
  • your precise or approximate location (the Game never requests location permission);
  • payment or financial information;
  • analytics, usage statistics or crash reports sent to us. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.

4. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the Game keeps a very small save file in the app's private storage on your device. It contains things such as your current level in each mode (Easy and Hard), your best level, the number of hints you hold, whether you have accepted this policy, and your settings (sound, vibration, reduced shake, reminders). It does not contain your name, any identifier or any personal information.

This file is never sent to us or to anyone else by the Game. It is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game. Because it is not backed up to us, we cannot restore it if it is lost.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

5.1 What ads you may see

The Game uses Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (and, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited). Ads appear in three ways only:

  • Banner ad on the home screen (and while the Game is loading);
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ad after you finish some later levels, shown on the result screen. Interstitials are not shown during the first levels, never during active play, and not more often than a fixed minimum interval;
  • Rewarded ad that you choose to watch by tapping a button (for example to receive an extra hint or an extra heart). A rewarded ad is never started without your tap.

5.2 What information Google may collect

To request, show, measure and secure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the Game may collect and process the following, according to Google's own policies:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and, where permitted, app-set or similar identifiers;
  • IP address, which may be used to infer a general (approximate) location, for example your country or region;
  • App interactions, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped;
  • Diagnostics and device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, network type and SDK performance information.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to limit how often the same ad is shown, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with legal requirements. Depending on your settings and region, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

5.3 Who is responsible

This information is collected and used by Google, which acts as an independent controller/provider for advertising under its own terms. Arb Studio does not receive your Advertising ID, and does not receive any profile of you from Google. We receive only aggregated advertising performance and earnings reports. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. AdMob's data practices are also described in AdMob help.

5.4 We do not sell your data

Arb Studio does not sell your personal information and does not share it with anyone for money. We do not use your data for advertising profiles of our own.

6. Your advertising choices and consent

  • Consent form. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where the law requires it, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management service) to ask for your consent before ads are requested, and lets you accept or decline personalised advertising. You can change that choice by clearing the Game's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Thread → Storage → Clear data), which shows the form again the next time you open the Game.
  • In-game acceptance. On first launch the Game shows a notice with a link to this policy; the advertising service is not started until you accept.
  • Personalised ads and Advertising ID. You can opt out of ad personalisation, or reset or delete your Advertising ID, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Google account controls. You can manage ad personalisation for your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Not interested in ads at all? Uninstalling the Game stops all advertising from it.

7. Reminder notifications

If you allow it, the Game can show occasional reminder notifications (for example, "Level 12 is waiting"). These notifications are created and scheduled locally on your device by Android's notification system. They do not use a server, are not based on personal data, and nothing about them is sent to us or to a third party.

The Game asks for notification permission once, after you have finished an early level, and only schedules reminders if you grant it. Reminders are not scheduled during night hours (10 PM – 9 AM) and are cancelled whenever you open the Game. You can switch them off at any time with the Reminders switch in the Game's Settings, or in Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Thread → Notifications.

8. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state – so that ads can be loaded and shown.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) – used by Google AdMob as described in section 5. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – used only for the optional reminders in section 7.
  • Vibration – used for optional haptic feedback in the Game; it can be turned off in Settings.

The Game does not request access to your location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, camera, microphone, phone or SMS.

9. Sharing of information

Arb Studio does not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not hold any to share. The only third party that receives data because of the Game is Google (through the AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform), as described above. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect our legal rights; as we hold no personal data about players, this is not expected to apply.

The Game is distributed through Google Play, which processes your information under the Google Privacy Policy when you download or update the Game.

10. International transfers

Google operates globally, so data processed for advertising may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Google relies on appropriate safeguards for such transfers (for example standard contractual clauses and other mechanisms described in its policies). Arb Studio itself does not transfer personal data because it does not collect any.

11. Retention

The save file described in section 4 stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game. Advertising data processed by Google is kept for the periods described in Google's own policies. Arb Studio keeps no personal data about players; correspondence you send us by e-mail is kept only as long as needed to answer you and is then deleted.

12. Security

We build the Game to work without collecting personal data, which is the strongest form of protection. The save file is protected by Android's application sandbox. Communication with advertising servers uses encrypted connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information handled by third parties.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws, or other applicable data-protection laws, you may have the right to know what data is processed about you, and to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of it, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data-protection authority.

Arb Studio holds no personal data about you, so we have nothing to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can exercise your choices through your Android settings, Google's ad settings and the privacy controls described in Google's Privacy Policy. You may also contact us and we will help direct your request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

"Do Not Sell or Share". We do not sell personal information. Advertising by Google may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising in some US states; you can opt out by turning off ad personalisation as described in section 6.
